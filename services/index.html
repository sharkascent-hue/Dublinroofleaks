Leak detection

How we trace a leak

Patching the ceiling stain almost never works, because water travels before it drops. Here’s how we find the real way in.

01

Start in the attic

Marks on the felt and timbers show the path the water took. We follow it back uphill to where it came in.

02

Check the usual suspects

Chimney junctions, flashings, valleys, vents and roof windows. We check the likely entry points rather than guessing from the ceiling.

03

Fix the detail, not the symptom

We repair or renew the failed slate, flashing or valley properly, instead of sealing over it with mastic.

04

Rule out everything else

If it’s condensation or plumbing, we’ll tell you so and point you to the right fix instead of charging for a roof repair.

Questions

Things people ask us

How quickly can you come out for a leak?
Emergency call-outs run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Ring us, describe what you can see, and we’ll give you the earliest response we can. If a permanent repair can’t be done on the first visit we’ll make the roof weathertight and book the follow-up.
Why isn’t the damp patch directly under the leak?
Water can run several metres along felt, battens and rafters before it drops through. The stain shows where the water left the roof structure, not where it got in. That’s why we start in the attic and at the roof details, not at the ceiling.
Could it be condensation rather than a leak?
Sometimes, especially in a poorly ventilated attic. If that’s what we find we’ll tell you and recommend ventilation rather than a roof repair you don’t need.
Should I wait for the rain to stop before calling?
No. Seeing a live leak is actually useful, and if water is actively coming in that’s an emergency call-out.
Do you charge for a quote?
No. Inspections and quotes are free and there’s no obligation. You’ll get a written price with photos of what we found, and nothing starts until you agree it.
Which areas do you cover?
All of Dublin city and county, from Swords and Malahide to Tallaght, Dún Laoghaire and Dalkey, plus work in Kildare, Meath and Wicklow. We’re based in Ballsbridge with two mobile teams.
Repair or replace: how do you decide?
If the roof structure, slates or tiles are sound and the leak is down to a flashing, valley, ridge or a few slipped slates, a repair is the right call. If the felt underneath has perished, the battens are rotten or you’re patching the same roof every winter, a re-roof usually works out better value. We’ll show you the evidence and let you decide.
Are you insured?
Yes. We carry full public liability insurance and can provide a copy on request.
Do you work with the council housing grants?
We do. We regularly carry out roofing under the Housing Aid for Older People scheme and can provide the itemised quotation and paperwork the council requires.
Do you give a guarantee?
Every job comes with a written guarantee on our workmanship, and where we install a manufacturer’s system (liquid roofing, GRP, membranes) you get their product guarantee as well.
