Roofers who fix leaks for a living, not as a sideline.

We’re a fully insured Dublin roofing crew that specialises in one thing: stopping water getting into your home. Two mobile teams cover Dublin city and county every day, with work in Kildare, Meath and Wicklow too.

Local authority grant work

Roof repairs through council housing grants.

If you or an older family member own the home you live in, essential roof repairs and re-roofing may qualify for support through the Housing Aid for Older People scheme run by your local authority.

We carry out grant-supported roofing work regularly. We’ll give you the itemised quotation the council needs, do the job to the required standard, and provide the paperwork for your claim.
