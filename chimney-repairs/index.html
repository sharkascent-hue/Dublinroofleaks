Why chimneys leak

Water gets in around the chimney, not through it.

Leaks usually start at the flashing, at the flaunching on top, or through worn mortar. We check each one and repair what has actually failed, instead of just treating the stain on the ceiling. If the chimney needs more than a repair, we show you why.
