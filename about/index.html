Who we are

A small crew with a simple rule: fix it properly, or don’t touch it.

Dublin Roof Leak Specialists started with leak call-outs, and that’s still the heart of what we do. Over the years that grew into flat roofs, slate and tile re-roofs, chimney work and new roofs on extensions.

We’re not a sales company with subcontractors. The people who quote your job are the people who do it. The same team also trades as Vantage Roofing, our second business, based in Ballsbridge with two mobile teams and emergency cover 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
